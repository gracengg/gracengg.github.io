Campus Energy Data & Investment Decisions

A proposal to connect building-level measurement, utility reporting and investment evaluation so campus energy decisions have a stronger evidence base.

Setting
Dickinson College, Center for Sustainability Education
Deliverable
Research report and phased implementation recommendations
Status
Proposal; no deployment or realized savings claimed

The decision

The April 2025 report identified limited building-level visibility and fragmented utility information as obstacles to evaluating energy performance. It asked how better measurement and connected reporting could support facilities planning, financial oversight and sustainability education.

My contribution

I synthesized peer-institution examples, compared the roles of utility-data platforms and proposed a phased path from metering to decision support. The recommendation connected data collection with investment tracking, staff training and ongoing data-quality checks.

Three complementary reporting needs

Platform consideredRole in the proposal
EnergyCAPUtility information, cost allocation and management reporting.
GRITSProject-level financial and environmental investment tracking.
ENERGY STAR Portfolio ManagerBuilding-performance benchmarking.

The brief describes how the 2025 proposal assigned these roles; it is not a current product evaluation.

Recommended sequence

  1. Define the decisions and measures. Agree on performance questions and the information needed to evaluate them.
  2. Pilot the measurement. Start with a manageable metering scope and validate the resulting data.
  3. Connect the reporting. Relate utility information to building context, project costs and performance comparisons.
  4. Build adoption and review. Train users, assign responsibility for data quality and expand after reviewing the pilot.

What the proposal establishes

The work demonstrates problem framing, comparative research and implementation planning. It does not establish that the institution adopted the recommendations or achieved the savings reported by peer institutions. Funding routes and vendor details discussed in the original report were time-specific and are not presented here as current commitments.

Source: Advancing Data-Driven Energy Decisions at Dickinson College, Grace Ngo, April 20, 2025. This page is an edited portfolio brief, not the original report.
