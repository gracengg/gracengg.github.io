Institutional Reporting & Alumni Engagement

A working CCLA alumni-engagement prototype connecting program participation with academic outcomes, access and geography.

Context
Institutional analytics internship
Tools
Excel, SQL and Power BI
Status
Intermediate CCLA source workbook; final dashboard not supplied

The reporting problem

Engagement records become more useful when they can be related to consistent academic, cohort and geographic definitions. A report must make those relationships understandable while preserving the privacy of individual alumni.

Scope supported by the working file

Engagement

Participation and recognition across several community-engagement activities.

Academic context

Graduation cohorts, fields of study and academic outcomes.

Access and geography

Context for comparing participation across relevant institutional groups and locations.

My broader internship contribution

My Dickinson work included reconciling and validating 15,000+ institutional records using SQL and Excel, developing Power BI reporting, conducting peer benchmarking and analyzing survey responses. This CCLA prototype is one workstream within that broader experience; the supplied workbook does not establish the content or completion of the full reporting suite.

Evidence and disclosure

This page is a scope brief, not a final dashboard demonstration. The supplied source is an intermediate version. Individual-level institutional records and small-group results are not published here, and no live dashboard is implied.

A final public case study would use an approved anonymized export or a clearly identified synthetic demonstration, with reporting definitions and any suppression rules reviewed first.
