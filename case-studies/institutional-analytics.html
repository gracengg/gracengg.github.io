Institutional Reporting & Alumni Engagement

A CCLA reporting prototype designed to connect community-engagement participation with academic outcomes, access and geography.

Context
Institutional analytics internship
Tools
Excel, SQL and Power BI
Deliverable
CCLA reporting prototype and analytical scope brief

The reporting problem

Engagement records become more useful when they can be related to consistent academic, cohort and geographic definitions. A report must make those relationships understandable while preserving the privacy of individual alumni.

A connected view of engagement

Engagement

Participation and recognition across several community-engagement activities.

Academic context

Graduation cohorts, fields of study and academic outcomes.

Access and geography

Context for comparing participation across relevant institutional groups and locations.

My broader internship contribution

My Dickinson work included cleaning, reconciling and validating 15,000+ records spanning 10+ years in SQL and Excel, building a 10-dashboard Power BI reporting suite, benchmarking 30+ peer institutions and analyzing Qualtrics survey responses. The CCLA prototype applies that reporting approach to a focused engagement question: how participation can be understood alongside academic and geographic context.

Value for institutional reporting

The design brings participation, cohort and academic context into a common reporting structure. It gives institutional teams a clearer basis for choosing comparison groups, reviewing coverage and discussing engagement patterns.

This public brief presents the reporting design and prototype scope. Institutional records remain within the institution.
